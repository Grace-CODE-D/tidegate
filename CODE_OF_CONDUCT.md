# Code of Conduct

Tidegate welcomes respectful, constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Participants should focus on the technical issue, assume good faith, give actionable feedback, and respect project boundaries. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when these standards are not followed. For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer privately through GitHub.
